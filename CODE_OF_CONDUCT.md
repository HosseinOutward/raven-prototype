# Code of Conduct

Raven exists because communication is vulnerable. People should be able to speak without being watched, pressured or performed at. This Code of Conduct applies that same belief to the people who build, host, use and discuss Raven.

## Scope

This code applies in every Raven project space: this repository's Issues, pull requests, Discussions and reviews, any community space run by the Raven collective, and anywhere a person speaks on Raven's behalf.

## Our standards

We expect everyone to:

- **Treat people as ends, not as means.** Each person is a person, not an audience, a lead or a vote.
- **Disagree honestly and with respect.** Say what you think and why. Argue against ideas, never against people.
- **Write clearly.** Make it easy for the next reader to understand you, whatever their first language.
- **Show restraint.** Keep to the topic, respect people's time, and accept that not every proposal will be adopted.
- **Respect privacy and consent.** Never share another person's personal information, private messages or conversations without their consent.
- **Own mistakes.** When you hurt someone, even by accident, listen, apologise and do better.

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, insults, threats or slurs, in any language, public or private.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background, body, beliefs or nationality.
- Sexual or violent content, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information (doxxing), or threatening to.
- Pressure campaigns: brigading, mass tagging, repeated demands on maintainers, or organising others to pile on.
- Sabotage of the project, its infrastructure or its contributors' work.
- Spam, and advertising unrelated to Raven.
- Entitlement and coercion toward people who give their time freely.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code:

- Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or discussion, or
- Contact the maintainers privately at **[CONTACT ADDRESS]**.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports. Report them privately through [Security › Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/raven-collective/raven-prototype/security/advisories/new).

Reports are read only by the maintainers handling them. We will keep the reporter's identity private unless they agree otherwise, and we will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith. A maintainer who is involved in a report takes no part in deciding it.

## Enforcement

Raven's moderation is meant to be visible, explained and proportionate. Maintainers decide which response fits, considering intent, impact and history:

1. **A note.** A private message explaining what was wrong. A public correction where the conversation needs one.
2. **A warning.** A recorded warning, with the consequence of continuing made clear. Content may be edited or hidden.
3. **A temporary restriction.** No participation in project spaces for a stated period.
4. **A permanent ban.** For sustained or severe harm, including any threat of violence or publishing private information.

When we act, we tell the person what we did and why, unless doing so would put the reporter at risk. Anyone may ask the maintainers to review a decision once, in writing.

## Relationship to the manifesto

This code puts into practice the contributor culture described in the [Raven Manifesto](docs/manifesto/Raven%20Manifesto%20v02.md#212-contributor-culture): technical excellence, clarity, restraint, user dignity, honest disagreement and respect for the product constitution, and no tolerance for harassment, sabotage, entitlement or opaque decision-making.

Changes to this code are proposed in Discussions and made by pull request.
