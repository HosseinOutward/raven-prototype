# Raven logo

**The idea:** a speech bubble that is also a raven's head. The bubble's tail is the beak; the eye is the only cut. One shape carries the name and what Raven is for: people talking.

**Construction:** a circle (the head, r = 72 on a 256 grid) and two tangents meeting at the beak tip: the top edge falls at 15°, the jaw rises at 45°. The eye is a circle of r = 10.5, 0.6 r forward and 0.36 r above the centre. The form is centred, then lifted 4 units (optical centre) and the tip let out 4 units (points read short). Everything is generated by [build.py](build.py); edit the numbers there, not the SVGs.

## Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live aqua | `#6EC4B8` | 110 196 184 | The app's `--live` token. Nearest Pantone 3248 C; CMYK approx. 55 0 30 0. Check against a printed swatch |
| Graphite | `#0F0E0D` | 15 14 13 | The app's `--background`. Print as rich black (60 40 40 100) |
| Bone | `#F0EEEA` | 240 238 234 | The app's `--foreground`, for type on dark grounds |

- **Aqua only on dark grounds.** Aqua on graphite is about 9.4:1; aqua on white is about 2:1 and too faint. On light grounds use graphite.
- Colour belongs to people in Raven's interface, so the mark is the only brand use of aqua. Don't spread it into chrome, buttons or backgrounds.
- One colour: graphite, black or white versions all carry the idea.

## Typography

The wordmark is Geist SemiBold (600) at −1 % tracking, converted to outlines, so it never depends on an installed font. Geist is under the SIL Open Font License, which allows logo use. Cap height is about half the head's diameter, and caps are centred on the head.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep one head radius (x) clear on every side. On the 256 grid that's 72.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px (use the small cut up to 24 px); horizontal lockup 96 px wide; stacked lockup 64 px tall. In print: symbol 5 mm, horizontal 25 mm.

## Don't

- Recolour the mark outside graphite, black, white and aqua, or put aqua on a light ground.
- Rotate or mirror it. It faces right, toward what's next.
- Add a gradient, outline, shadow or glow.
- Fill in the eye, or add a mouth, wing or feet.
- Stretch it, or change the space between the symbol and the wordmark.
- Retype the wordmark in a live font.
- Put it on busy photos without a solid graphite or white plate.
