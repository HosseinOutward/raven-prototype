## What is true now

All paths are under `apps/desktop`. Line numbers are from 2026-10-18, before plan 01;
verify them.

- **The speaking ring is loudness only.**
  - `src/lib/speakingActivity.ts` lights a person when the RMS level crosses
    `SPEAKING_ENTER_RMS_DB = -52` and clears below `-56` after a 240 ms hold. Remote
    tracks use the received pre-fader level (`inputMeter ?? meter`); local sources use
    the strip meter.
  - Levels arrive through `audio-meter-changed` every 120 ms (`AudioMeterUpdate`,
    `src-tauri/src/audio/domain.rs:428`).
- **"Talking while muted" is loudness only, too.** `src/lib/mutedSpeech.ts` uses a
  higher threshold (`-42 dB`, three loud readings in a second) to avoid keyboards. That
  also makes it miss quiet speech.
- **A cheap voice detector is already compiled into Raven.** sonora's AGC2 runs WebRTC's
  RNN VAD. The `sonora-agc2` crate (BSD-3, v0.1.0, already in `Cargo.lock`) exposes it
  publicly:
  - `sonora_agc2::vad_wrapper::VoiceActivityDetectorWrapper::new(backend, sample_rate_hz)`
    with `analyze(&mut self, frame: &[f32]) -> f32`;
  - the input is the first channel of one 10 ms frame at the given rate, and the output
    is a speech probability in 0..1;
  - it resamples internally and resets itself periodically;
  - the backend comes from `sonora_simd::detect_backend()`.

  It is much cheaper than running RNNoise, so it can run on every incoming track.
- **The remote reader** (`src-tauri/src/audio/engine.rs`, around lines 1166–1290) gets
  10 ms frames of stereo i16 at the output rate from `NativeAudioStream`. Per frame it:
  1. accumulates the received pre-fader level (`input_level`);
  2. applies the receiver gain and mute with a slew;
  3. runs the listener's processor chain;
  4. pushes the result to the `OutputMixer`.

  Every 40 ms it refreshes its controls with `try_receiver_snapshot` (`engine.rs:1893`),
  which returns `(gain, muted, chain)`. Every 120 ms it emits `update_remote_meter`.
- **Receiver strips** are `RemoteChannelStripState` (`domain.rs:701`):
  - They are remembered across reconnects by track, then by participant and source kind
    (`remember_remote_strip`, `state.rs:851`).
  - Only the gain reaches disk, through `RememberedVolume { participant_id, source_type,
    gain_db }` in `remembered-volumes.json` (`state.rs:139`, `3298`–`3320`).
- **Per-person controls in the UI.**
  - A person's *microphone* volume and mute for you are only in the Mixer (remote strips
    in `src/components/AudioMixerPanel.tsx`).
  - The stage's `SatelliteCluster.tsx` popover ("Volume for you", "Mute for me", "Only
    you hear this change.") is only for a person's extra sources, not their mic.
- **Processing switches:** echo cancellation and automatic gain are in the dock's mic
  popover and the Mixer. They reset on restart: `MediaSettings` in
  `useRavenMediaAdapter.ts` (`readMediaSettings`, around line 1320) stores the mic device
  and, after plan 01, the suppression mode, but not these two.
- **Settings categories** are listed in `src/shells/ravenv1/UserSettings.tsx` (lines
  44–66): the "System" group holds Server and Updates. There is no About or licences
  screen. `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` from plan 01 covers only the suppression crates.

## A. Voice detection for the speaking ring and the muted warning

**Engine:**

- Add `sonora-agc2` and `sonora-simd` as direct dependencies, at exactly the versions
  sonora already pulls in. Wrap `VoiceActivityDetectorWrapper` in a small
  `VoiceDetector` (new file `src-tauri/src/audio/voice.rs`) that:
  - takes interleaved f32 blocks of any size at the stream rate and frames them into
    10 ms itself (sources with every processing switch off are not framed by the APM);
  - analyses channel 0;
  - keeps the **highest** probability seen since the last read, and resets it on read.
    The meter window is 120 ms, and a syllable shorter than that must still count.
- **Local microphone:** run it in `process_source_samples`:
  - after the suppressor stage and the back APM, before the fader, so it still works
    while muted;
  - on microphones only.
- **Remote tracks:** run it in the reader on the received audio, before the receiver
  gain and before the cleanup in B, next to `input_level`. That keeps it independent of
  this listener's volume and mute, as `inputMeter` is today. Convert from i16 once.
  Microphone tracks only (read the track's source type).
- **Report:** add `voice_probability: Option<f32>` to `AudioMeterUpdate`, plus its twin
  in `src/lib/audio.ts`. Fill it on local microphone updates (muted or not) and on
  remote microphone updates.
- **Privacy:**
  - It is computed on this machine from audio this machine already has, and is never
    sent anywhere.
  - Say so in a doc comment, as `input_meter`'s does. It answers "is someone speaking",
    which the ring already shows. It is not emotion or behaviour analysis (manifesto
    §18).

**UI logic** (`speakingActivity.ts`, `mutedSpeech.ts`):

- A person starts speaking when the level is over the enter threshold **and**
  `voiceProbability >= 0.6`. They stop when the level drops below the exit threshold
  **or** `voiceProbability < 0.3`, after the existing hold. Intensity still comes from
  the level.
- When `voiceProbability` is missing (an older peer's meter, or a non-mic source), keep
  today's behaviour.
- The muted warning counts a reading as speech only when `voiceProbability >= 0.6`.
  Lower its threshold to about `-48 dB`: voice detection now rejects keyboards, so it no
  longer has to rely on loudness. Tune it on the rig and say what you settled on.
- Put the thresholds in named constants beside the existing ones, and extend
  `speakingActivity.test.ts` and `mutedSpeech.test.ts`: loud non-voice doesn't light up,
  quiet voice does, and a missing probability falls back.

## C. Suppression depth

- Add a `NoiseSuppressionDepth { Gentle, Balanced, Full }` setting next to the mode.
  `Balanced` is the default and equals plan 01's 24 dB constant. Map each step to every
  mode:

  | Depth | Standard (sonora level) | Strong (dry mix floor) | Studio (plan 02, `atten_lim_db`) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Gentle | `Low` (~6 dB) | 12 dB | 12 dB |
  | Balanced | `Moderate` (~12 dB) | 24 dB | 24 dB |
  | Full | `VeryHigh` (~21 dB) | no dry mix | 100 (no limit) |

  Standard maps to sonora's own levels because that's the control it has. Changing the
  level goes through `apply_config` and resets only the NS submodule.
- **Wire:** add `noise_suppression_depth` to `WebRtcAudioProcessingSettings` and its TS
  twin.
- **Live:** carry it through `LiveControls` like the mode. Strong changes its dry-mix
  gain with a short ramp. Remote cleanup (B) reads the same depth.
- **Remember:** store it in `MediaSettings` next to the mode.
- **UI:** in the Mixer only, under the suppression select of the local mic strip, add a
  three-step control labelled "How much to remove". Use the segmented control from
  `components/ui/tabs.tsx`, or whatever plan 01 used for the mode, to stay consistent.
  Steps: "A little", "Balanced", "As much as possible". Caption for Full: "Silence
  between words may sound unnatural." Not in the dock popover.
